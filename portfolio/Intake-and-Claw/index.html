Intake and Claw Design

FTC Robotics 2023–2024 — Autodesk Fusion

One challenge of the 2023–2024 FTC season was to collect pyramid-shaped game elements arranged in random orientations. Our robot began with a passive intake, but it had a weak grip when clipping and was inconsistent with pieces in arbitrary orientations.

I designed a custom claw: a gearbox-driven actuator with induced-fit, pyramid-shaped fingers optimized for form-fit contact, mounted on a servo-driven mechanism that provides three degrees of freedom (rotation about the x, y and z axes) to orient the claw. The geometry improved contact reliability and scoring efficiency and was integrated into the final robot.
