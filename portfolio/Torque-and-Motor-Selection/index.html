Torque and Motor Selection

FTC Robotics — hardware analysis

When selecting linear actuator motors, I applied physics and calculus to compute the minimum required torque instead of relying on trial and error.

Given subsystem mass m and required acceleration a, F = ma. For a lead screw of effective radius rs and mechanical efficiency η < 1, the torque requirement is τ = m·a·rs / η.

I computed efficiency curves and matched gearbox ratios to find the best torque-speed balance, settling on a 435 RPM motor that minimizes losses and heat.
