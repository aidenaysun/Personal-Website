Odometry-Based Autonomous Pathing

FTC Robotics — used in the 2023–2025 seasons

Our early autonomous used timed, stop-and-go position drives that were slow and imprecise. I replaced them by logging the robot's global pose (x, y, θ) from odometry and IMU data, plotting those runs, and generating optimized paths from the plots. A continuous drive-to-position controller then follows the path, which cut runtime and improved precision.

Tools: Android Studio, Java, OpenCV, TensorFlow.
