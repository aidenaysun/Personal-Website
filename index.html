Hello, I'm Aiden

AI & Decision Making · MIT ’30

I’m studying AI & Decision Making at MIT. I build things at the intersection of machine learning, software, and robotics, with an interest in turning ideas into systems that work in the real world.

My work spans machine learning research, full-stack applications, and robot design. I’m currently a machine learning researcher with MIT Sea Grant, working on tools for fisheries and aquaculture.

What interests me

I’m interested in how machines sense, learn, and make decisions. I enjoy exploring machine learning and control for robotics, building useful software, and connecting physical hardware with intelligent systems.

I like projects that bring different disciplines together, from spectral sensing and computer vision to autonomous motion and mechanical design.
